Delivery & policies

Delivery

Based at Venkatesh Graffiti, Keshav Nagar, Mundhwa, Pune, Maharashtra. Local delivery is available in Keshav Nagar, Mundhwa; Magarpatta City, Hadapsar; Amanora Park Town, Hadapsar; and Kharadi.

Availability

Items shown on this website are subject to confirmation. Product availability is confirmed on WhatsApp only.

Exchange, returns & cancellation

Exchange, return and cancellation requests must be discussed on WhatsApp. Exchanges are available only against other available items, subject to availability; an exact item exchange cannot be guaranteed.

Privacy & website notice

Your bag and recent WhatsApp checkouts are stored only in this browser; checkout history is kept for seven days. The website cannot confirm whether a WhatsApp message was sent or an order was accepted.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your bag and select Place order to send the order details on WhatsApp. We will confirm availability and next steps there.

Can I collect my order?

Yes. Select Self pickup in the bag. Pickup details can be discussed on WhatsApp.

How are delivery charges calculated?

Delivery charges are separate and based on the actual charge for the order. Confirm the final amount on WhatsApp before placing the order.

Can I exchange a purchased item?

Exchange requests are discussed individually on WhatsApp. Exchanges are offered only for other available items, not a guaranteed exchange of the exact item.
